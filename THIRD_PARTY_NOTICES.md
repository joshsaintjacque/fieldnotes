# Third-party notices

## Open-Meteo

Field Notes requests weather, geocoding, and air-quality data from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/). Open-Meteo data is attributed in the weather panel as required by its [CC BY 4.0 license](https://open-meteo.com/en/license). The free API is for non-commercial use and is rate limited; review the current [terms of use](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms) before other use.

US Air Quality Index data is provided through Open-Meteo and sourced from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS).
